The rule
  1. Black and White take turns placing a stone.
  2. Three in a row wins: across, down or diagonal.
  3. Board full with no line: draw.

The AI is told only this rule. It starts out playing randomly.

The math, if you want it

Choosing where to look (PUCT)
a = argmax [ Q(s,a) + U(s,a) ]
U = c · P(s,a) · √ΣN / (1 + N(s,a))

Q: what has worked. U: liked by the network but rarely tried.

Turning visits into a move
π(a) ∝ N(s,a)^(1/τ)

Early moves are sampled for variety (τ = 1); later, the most-visited move is played.

The training goal (loss)
l = (z − v)² − πᵀ log p + c‖θ‖²

Predict the winner, copy the search, keep weights small.
